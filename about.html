About CreateLK AL

CreateLK AL is a digital learning platform designed to help Sri Lankan G.C.E. Advanced Level students practise and improve their academic knowledge through online learning tools.

Our Focus

Our initial focus is G.C.E. A/L Biology, with topic-based MCQs, AI-assisted practice, progress tracking and examination preparation resources.

Our Goal

Our goal is to make regular academic practice more accessible, structured and convenient for students.

Learning Approach

Students can practise at their own pace, review their performance and continue building their knowledge through regular practice.

Future Subjects

Additional A/L subjects may be introduced as the platform grows.
